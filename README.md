### Building production AI systems from an engineering-first mindset

</div>

---

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer Intern at NEOTEX.ai** (Brussels, remote), where I ship OpenAI/Gemini-powered features to production: from optimizing serving latency for the Halisi Livestock mobile app to building diffusion-based synthetic-data and vision-language pipelines. I'm also a **PyTorch Ambassador** with the Linux Foundation, organizing deep-learning events across Sub-Saharan Africa and contributing to the PyTorch/ExecuTorch open-source ecosystem.

My background is in **mechanical engineering** (BSc, Distinction). That foundation still shows up in how I approach systems, physics, and hardware-aware ML. Today my work centers on **multi-agent LLM systems, computer vision, geospatial ML, and physics-informed neural networks**, with published research bridging the two worlds.

- 🔬 **Published research:** *Development of a Thermoacoustically-Driven Thermoacoustic Refrigerator* (2025, peer-reviewed): 70% Carnot efficiency with zero-GWP working gases, validated through computational modeling.
- 🌍 **Community:** Organiser of **Deep Learning IndabaX DRC**, growing the AI ecosystem across Central & East Africa.
- 🧠 **Currently exploring:** agentic architectures, on-device inference with ExecuTorch, and satellite-imagery ML for climate resilience.
- 📍 Based in Kampala, Uganda (EAT).
